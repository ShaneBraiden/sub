# Chapter markers: topic segmentation results

Generated by `experiments/eval_chapters.py`. Test documents: 200 joins of 3-6 excerpts (90-300 s, cut at sentence ends) of different test recordings (16 LibriSpeech chapters, 10 TED talks); each junction is a true chapter boundary and its pause is drawn from the pauses between sentences. Mean length 14.5 min, 4.5 chapters. Settings were tuned on 80 documents built the same way from the dev recordings (Mode B words, lowest WindowDiff). Every method keeps chapters at least 60 s long. Pk and WindowDiff are error rates over words (k = half the mean true chapter length); P/R/F1 count a predicted chapter start as correct within ±10 s of an unmatched true one. Bootstrap CIs resample documents (documents share recordings, so they are optimistic).

Chosen: **dp** with `['dp', 'NVA', 0.0, 6.0]`; TextTiling best `['tiling', 'NVAR', 0.0, 400, 4, 0.0]`, DP best `['dp', 'NVA', 0.0, 6.0]` (key = method, content words, generic_logp, parameters).
